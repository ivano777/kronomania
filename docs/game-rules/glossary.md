# Glossary

## Action
Any gameplay operation that produces an outcome through the rules engine.

## Burnout
A magical state entered when Fervor would rise above the caster's **current modified Ingenuity** cap.
Burnout is checked **after** the spell that caused the overflow resolves.
Default effects:
- true spells are blocked;
- cantrips remain available;
- Fervor is clamped at the cap and stays there until recovery changes it.

## Cantrip
A minor magical action unlocked through **Minor Studies**.
Cantrips do not use Fervor, do not escalate Fervor, follow normal timing and Guard rules, and remain available during Burnout.

## Defense Pool
One of the three defensive roll families:
- **Stance**
- **Resolve**
- **Stamina**

Each defense pool can normally be rolled once per turn.
Once rolled, it creates a Guard reserve that is then consumed by matching hostile pressure.

## Defeat
The state entered when a character's accumulated Wounds >= their Max Wounds.
Player characters have Max Wounds = 3 by default; some nodes may increase this.

## Flat
A fixed numeric modifier applied after Keep.

## Fervor Die (Real)
A real Fervor die is granted by the Spellcasting node or later features.
It is rolled **after** the normal pool has been kept, added directly to the final total, and cannot be discarded.
It is Fervor-tagged: if it rolls its maximum, Fervor escalates by 1 step after spell resolution.

## Fervor Die (Substitution)
A substitution die is a normal pool die converted to an Ingenuity-based die by a spell or feature.
It is rolled with the normal pool, subject to normal Keep, and may be discarded.
It is still Fervor-tagged: if it rolls its maximum (even if discarded), Fervor escalates by 1 step.

## Guard
A temporary reserve created by a defense roll.
Guard is not a passive stat.

## Ingenuity
One of the three core stats.
Its stable Size is used for Fervor containment and cantrip knowledge.

## Keep
The number of rolled dice that may be selected and summed.

## Long Rest
A full recovery state that removes Burnout and resets Fervor to base Size.

## Massive Result
A stronger outcome triggered when:
**(final total - relevant Guard) > relevant defensive Size**
where "relevant defensive Size" is the numeric face value of the triggered defensive stat (e.g., d8 = 8, d10 = 10).
Default consequence for direct harm: 2 Wounds instead of 1.

## Minor Studies
The Training node family that unlocks cantrip use and increases the number of known cantrips.

## Pool
The full set of dice rolled before Keep is applied.

## Recovery Scene
A non-combat recovery event that removes Burnout but does not reduce current Fervor Size.

## Size
The die size used by a stat or effect.
Normal stable progression: d4, d6, d8, d10.
Exceptional / temporary only: d2, d12.

## Stable Size
A character's persistent stat value, ignoring temporary buffs, debuffs, scene effects, or short-term state changes.

## Stamina
The defensive expression of Dominion.

## Stance
The defensive expression of Negation.

## Tier
The main progression term for base pool size.

## True Spell
A spell that uses the Fervor subsystem.

## Velocity Threshold (VT)
The threshold that determines whether an action resolves in the Fast or Slow phase.

## Wound
A major harm unit suffered when Guard is broken or massively overrun.

# Soft CC

Soft CC covers control-oriented effects that hinder or suppress targets without defaulting immediately to full turn denial.

## Standard Success
On ordinary success, a control effect usually applies a tactical debuff.

Examples:
- become Slow
- suffer disadvantage on attacks
- lose access to a specific option
- become partially impaired

## Massive Success
On a **massive result**, the stronger form of the control may apply.

Examples:
- full sleep collapse
- forced retreat
- complete restraint
- severe action suppression

Use the standard massive-threshold definition (see [Wounds and Massive Damage](./wounds-and-massive-damage.md)):
**(final total - relevant Guard) > relevant defensive Size**

## Shared Engine
Soft CC:
- follows VT timing
- targets the appropriate defense pool
- fails if it does not overcome Guard

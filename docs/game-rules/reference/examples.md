# Examples

## Example 1: Physical Attack
Tier 2, Dominion d8, Keep 2:
- build 2d8
- roll [3, 7]
- keep 7 + 3 = 10
- add Flat if any
- compare against Guard

## Example 2: Spell with Real Fervor Die
Tier 2 true spell, Keep 2, one real Fervor die (d4):
- build normal pool: 2 dice
- roll normal pool: [5, 6]
- Keep 2 from normal pool: 5 + 6 = 11
- roll real Fervor die (additive, post-keep): [3]
- final total: 11 + 3 = 14
- compare 14 against Guard

## Example 2b: Spell with Substitution Die
Tier 2 true spell, Keep 2, one substitution die (one normal die converted to Ingenuity d6):
- build normal pool: 1 normal die + 1 substitution die (Ingenuity d6, Fervor-tagged)
- roll pool: normal [5], substitution [6]
- Keep 2 from pool: 5 + 6 = 11
- substitution die rolled 6 (its max) -> Fervor escalates 1 step after resolution, even though it was kept
- final total: 11
- compare 11 against Guard

## Example 3: Burnout Trigger
Current modified Ingenuity d8 (Fervor cap = 8, i.e., d8), current Fervor at d8:
- cast spell
- real Fervor die rolls 8 (its maximum)
- spell resolves
- Fervor escalation would move to d10, which exceeds the d8 cap
- Burnout begins; Fervor is clamped at d8

## Example 4: Recovery Scene
Burnout at d10:
- Recovery Scene removes Burnout
- Fervor remains d10

# FAQ

## Does failing VT mean failure?
No. It usually means the action is Slow.

## Do cantrips auto-succeed?
No. They still need to overcome Guard.

## Are cantrips blocked by Burnout?
No.

## Does the spell that triggers Burnout still resolve?
Yes.

## Can the same defense pool be rolled again in the same turn?
Not by default. Once rolled, it absorbs further same-type pressure without re-rolling.

## What resets Fervor fully?
A Long Rest.

## What is stable Size?
The persistent stat value, ignoring temporary modification.

## Is the Fervor cap based on stable or current Ingenuity?
Current modified Ingenuity. If Ingenuity is reduced, the cap drops immediately and Fervor is clamped to it.

## Does Fervor clamping from a reduced Ingenuity remove Burnout?
No. Clamping is not recovery. Burnout requires a Long Rest or Recovery Scene to remove.

## If a substitution die rolls max but is discarded, does Fervor still escalate?
Yes. Any Fervor-tagged die that rolls its maximum contributes to escalation, even if discarded from the normal Keep.

## What stat do physical attacks use by default?
Dominion (offense). Negation defends against them via Stance by default.

## What is the default mental or control stat?
Ingenuity.

## When is a result Massive?
When (final total - relevant Guard) > relevant defensive Size (numeric face of the defensive stat, e.g., d8 = 8).

## What happens when a player character accumulates Max Wounds?
They are Defeated. Default Max Wounds = 3; nodes may increase it.

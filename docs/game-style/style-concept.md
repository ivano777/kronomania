# Game Concept: 2D Tactical Duel RPG

## 1. The Vision
A 2D turn-based tactical RPG focused on intense, strategic duels. Deeply inspired by classic tabletop role-playing games (like D&D), the game translates the thrill of dice rolls, deep character building, and dynamic equipment management into a highly rewarding digital experience. It is a game about preparation, tactical choices, and the unpredictability of battle.

## 2. Setting & Atmosphere
- **World & Lore:** A gritty, dark-fantasy setting where combatants—ranging from seasoned warriors to arcane spellcasters—face off in deadly encounters. Whether in underground arenas, ruined dungeons, or forgotten temples, survival relies on skill and preparation.
- **Tone:** Tense, strategic, and rewarding. Every choice made outside of combat impacts survival, and every turn in combat carries weight.
- **Art Style (Proposed):** High-quality 2D visuals (e.g., stylized hand-drawn art or detailed dark-fantasy pixel art). The environments are atmospheric and moody.
- **UI Design:** Clean, modern, and unobtrusive. Menus like the Skill Tree and Character Sheet are designed to feel like authentic, tangible RPG documents, keeping the player immersed in the role-playing experience.

## 3. Core Gameplay Loop
The game flows through a satisfying cycle of preparation and execution:
1. **The Hub (Preparation):** The safe zone. Here, players manage their *Character Sheet*, rest to heal wounds, and plan their ultimate build.
2. **Progression:** Players spend earned Skill Points in a sprawling, visually engaging Skill Tree to unlock new moves or upgrade existing ones.
3. **Loadout & Strategy:** Equipping specific weapons, spells, or unarmed styles. Equipment synergizes with abilities through a "Tag" system (e.g., equipping a [Blade] makes slashing skills deadlier).
4. **The Duel:** Entering the dungeon or arena to face enemies in turn-based combat.
5. **Reward:** Earning experience, discovering new tools, and returning to the Hub to grow stronger.

## 4. Progression System
- **The Skill Tree:** A massive, interconnected web of abilities. Players don't just level up automatically; they chart their own path. Nodes represent active attacks, defensive stances, or passive buffs, and many abilities can be mastered across multiple levels.
- **Classless Freedom:** The player's playstyle is defined by the abilities they choose to unlock and the tools they equip, rather than being locked into rigid traditional classes. 

## 5. Combat System (High-Level)
*Note: The detailed mechanics, formulas, and rules of engagement will be documented in a separate `Combat_System.md` file.*

- **Turn-Based Tactics:** Battles are one-on-one (or small scale) turn-based affairs where pacing and resource management are key.
- **Dice-Based Resolution:** Actions are resolved through simulated dice rolls, capturing the tabletop RPG feel. Your character's proficiency dictates how many dice are rolled and kept.
- **Active Defenses:** Dodging or absorbing hits isn't just a static armor number. Characters rely on dynamic defensive pools (like Stamina or Resolve) that must be broken down by the attacker.
- **Momentum & Crits:** Critical successes can trigger snowballing mechanics (like magical "Fervour"), making the combat feel alive, swingy, and highly dramatic.

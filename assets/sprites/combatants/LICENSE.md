# Combatant sprite licenses

All combatant animation sheets in this directory are converted from asset
packs by **LuizMelo**, released under **CC0 (Creative Commons Zero v1.0
Universal)** — free for commercial and non-commercial use, no attribution
required (credited here with gratitude anyway).

| Folder | Pack | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `player/` | Martial Hero | https://luizmelo.itch.io/martial-hero |
| `grunt/` | Monsters & Creatures Fantasy (Goblin) | https://luizmelo.itch.io/monsters-creatures-fantasy |
| `soldier/` | Medieval Warrior Pack 2 | https://luizmelo.itch.io/medieval-warrior-pack-2 |
| `knight/` | Hero Knight | https://luizmelo.itch.io/hero-knight |

Conversion (strip slicing, union-bbox crop, optional mirroring, fps/loop
metadata) is performed by `tools/sprites/import_pack.py` driven by
`tools/sprites/packs.json`. Original ZIPs are not committed
(`tools/sprites/packs/` is gitignored); re-download from the links above.
